Privacy Policy

AudioLab RTA Analyzer does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data.

  • The app uses the microphone or the connected audio interface only to analyze sound in real time on your device. Audio is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Calibration files, snapshots and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and makes no network connections.
  • No account is required.
