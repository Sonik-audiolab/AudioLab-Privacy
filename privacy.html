Privacy Policy

Overview

Rider Builder PRO AudioLab does not collect, transmit, store or share any personal data.

Data Collection

The app does not collect any data. All information you enter (Cover, Input List, Stage Plot, Backline & Notes) is stored locally on your device only.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, cookies or tracking tools.

Internet Connection

The app works 100% offline. No internet connection is required and no data is sent to external servers.

PDF Export

PDF files are generated locally on your device. They are saved only where you choose to save them.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: audiolabtenerife@gmail.com

This policy applies to the iOS and macOS versions of Rider Builder PRO AudioLab available on the Apple App Store.
